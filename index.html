Welcome to Quiet

A calm place to get thoughts out of your head and gently reset. No streaks, no scores, no guilt — come as often or as little as you need. There's no wrong way to use this.

🧠

Two ways to write

🧠
Quick Dump — racing thoughts out fast, one line each. Then sort them into Do / Decide / Let go / Just feelings with a tap.
✍️
Free Journal — a real diary page for when you want to write properly. Flip back through past pages anytime, like a real book.
🌀
Flooded, or stuck on a looping thought? Calm a Spiral walks you down gently — breathe, then wind it down to one small step. It's saved to History too.
✨

Catch the good stuff

✨
Drop tiny wins in the Win Jar — showered, replied, drank water — and watch it fill up.
📖
Everything you write is saved to History, with a gentle look at how you've been. A kind mirror, never a report card.
🔒

Yours, and only yours

Everything you write stays right here on your device — private, and never uploaded anywhere.

💾
The one little habit that keeps it safe: tap Export in History now and then to save a backup file (pop it in your cloud drive).
📂
New phone or browser? Just Import that file and everything's back. We'll give you a gentle nudge when it's a good time to back up.
